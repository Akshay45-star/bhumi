Project Overview

What is Bhumi?

Bhumi is an academic research project and mobile application that investigates the intersection of personal memory preservation, digital estate planning, and family communication. The project explores a fundamental human question: what do we leave behind for the people we love?

The application allows users to record daily 30-second video check-ins, create time-locked milestone messages for future delivery, document financial assets for family awareness, and invite family members to receive messages through a secure private delivery system.

Bhumi is not a financial planning service, legal estate service, or death verification system. It is a personal communication and memory tool built on top of modern mobile and cloud infrastructure.

Research Questions

What We're Studying

  • How do people want to communicate with family members after their passing, and what formats feel most authentic?
  • Can a time-lock inactivity mechanism serve as a practical proxy for life events without requiring death verification?
  • What is the user experience of creating legacy content — does the act of recording itself have psychological value?
  • How do families receive posthumous digital messages, and what emotional and practical impact do they have?
  • What are the ethical boundaries of "deadman switch" mechanisms in consumer mobile applications?
  • How can financial asset documentation be presented to lay users in a way that reduces family stress during bereavement?

Worst-Case Scenario Analysis

Risk Register & Mitigations

As an academic project handling sensitive personal legacy data, we document all foreseeable failure modes and their mitigations transparently.

CRITICAL
Messages deliver while user is alive and active
A user sets a 30-day inactivity window, travels without internet for 35 days, and their milestone messages unlock and deliver to family while they are alive. Family receives "goodbye" messages causing severe emotional distress and family conflict.
✓ Mitigation: 2 advance push notifications at 80% and 95% of window. Users acknowledge this risk explicitly at setup. Terms of Service Section 4 makes this risk clear. Users are advised to set windows of 90+ days minimum.
CRITICAL
Service shutdown — messages never delivered
The company stops paying for Railway or Neon. The API goes offline. Users who recorded messages for their families expecting future delivery — those messages are never sent. Users pass away and families receive nothing.
✓ Mitigation: Database lives on Neon free tier (no payment required). Videos on Neon Object Storage. Code is open-sourced on GitHub. Users can export all data from Settings. 30-day notice policy before any shutdown.
CRITICAL
Data breach — private legacy videos exposed
A security vulnerability exposes presigned video URLs or database credentials. Private, emotionally sensitive videos recorded for families are accessed by unauthorized parties.
✓ Mitigation: Presigned URLs expire in 7 days. Videos are not publicly indexed. JWT tokens expire. OTP codes hash-stored and purged in 24hrs. TLS in transit, encryption at rest via Neon.
HIGH
Wrong recipient receives a message
User invites a family member with a typo in their email. Someone else receives deeply personal legacy messages intended for a child or spouse.
✓ Mitigation: Recipient preview screen shown before invite is sent. Confirmation required. Users can delete family connections at any time before delivery.
HIGH
Harmful content recorded and delivered
A user records abusive, threatening, or psychologically harmful messages intended to hurt family members after the user's passing. Recipients have no way to refuse delivery.
✓ Mitigation: Terms of Service Section 6 prohibits harmful content. Recipients can contact support@bhumi.app to report and block delivery. This is a known ethical limitation of all legacy platforms.
HIGH
Financial asset data used as evidence in disputes
A user documents family assets in Bhumi. During a legal dispute (divorce, inheritance), a court subpoenas Bhumi's records. The asset notes — which are informal and unverified — are used as evidence.
✓ Mitigation: Terms of Service Section 5 explicitly states asset data is "personal notes only, not verified." Privacy Policy Section 2 clarifies this is equivalent to a personal diary. We comply with lawful court orders.
MEDIUM
Streak anxiety causes unhealthy app dependency
The streak mechanic creates compulsive checking behaviour. Users feel anxious when they miss a day. The emotional weight of the app's framing ("your family needs this") causes psychological distress.
✓ Mitigation: Streak freeze feature allows guilt-free breaks. No notifications after 10pm. Users can disable notifications in Settings. This is a documented risk in gamified wellness apps.
MEDIUM
Account hacked — messages deleted or altered
An attacker gains access to a user's account and deletes all recorded messages, or worse, records replacement messages impersonating the user.
✓ Mitigation: Email OTP + Apple Sign In (no passwords stored). Message PIN for family inbox access. Future: message signing/hash verification planned.
MEDIUM
Minor receives adult legacy messages prematurely
A user records a message "for my daughter on her 18th birthday" but sets a 90-day inactivity trigger. The user passes away when the daughter is 12. The message delivers to a minor.
✓ Mitigation: Recipients receive a web player link via email — they must actively choose to open it. No messages auto-play. Privacy Policy Section 7 addresses minor recipients. Manual trigger option available.
Ethical Framework

Design Principles

No AI determines outcomes

Bhumi uses a simple inactivity timer — not AI, not biometric data, not death certificates. The user sets the window. The user is warned. The user is in control at all times.

Data minimisation

We collect name, email, and recordings. Nothing else. No location. No contacts. No biometrics. No behavioural tracking. No advertising.

Right to be forgotten

Account deletion permanently removes all data within 7 days. No backups retained after deletion. This is irreversible by design.

Transparency over engagement

We document all worst-case scenarios publicly on this page. We do not hide the risks of time-lock delivery. We believe informed users make better decisions.
